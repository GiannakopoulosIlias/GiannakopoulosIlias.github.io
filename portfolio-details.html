Computational Electromagnetics

An overview of my work on RF‑coil modeling and integral‑equation solvers.

Computational electromagnetics lies at the heart of modern MRI coil design. My software implements wire, surface and volume integral‐equation methods accelerated by tensor decompositions and fast solvers. Below are a few illustrative snapshots from recent projects.

Wire‐Volume Solver Performance

We benchmarked our VIE solver on 64×64×64 grids, achieving a 10× speedup over the standard MoM approach while preserving S‑parameter accuracy.

  • Tensor‐train compression of interaction matrices
  • Adaptive cross approximation for near‐field kernels

Surface‐Integral Field Maps

Simulated B1+ maps for a head‐sized phantom at 7 T demonstrate the characteristic interference patterns.
